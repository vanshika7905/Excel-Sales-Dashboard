📊 Sales & Customer Behavior Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze customer behavior, purchase patterns, delivery performance, and satisfaction levels.
This project focuses on understanding how customers buy, what they buy, where they live, and how satisfied they are.

🔍 Project Overview

This dashboard answers key business questions such as:

How are sales and orders trending over time?

Which channels do customers prefer to shop from?

What products are popular by gender?

How quickly are orders delivered?

How satisfied are customers month-over-month?

The dashboard enables data-driven decisions through intuitive visuals and filters.
